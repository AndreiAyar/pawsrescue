# Community expectations

We want people with different backgrounds and levels of experience to be able
to help. Be respectful, explain your reasoning, and give feedback about the work.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, and publishing
someone else's private information are not welcome. Avoid sharing identifiable
pet-owner reports when discussing a bug; use fictional examples instead.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and restrict participation when
needed. For a public incident, flag the relevant issue or pull request to
the maintainer. For sensitive concerns, ask for a private contact channel without
posting personal details. GitHub's own abuse reporting is also available.

These expectations apply to this repository's issues, reviews, and discussions.
Participation is voluntary; respectful disagreement is welcome.
